About Me

Bridging logic, creativity, and modern software engineering.

My name is Nikoly Beatriz Schmaeller Loch. I am a passionate Junior Full-Stack Software Developer. I specialize in designing scalable backend architectures, relational database management, and responsive front-end user experiences.

I graduated from a technical high school and am currently in my second year pursuing a higher degree in Software Development (TSDS - Tecnicatura Superior en Desarrollo de Software). My foundation covers hands-on full-stack development, object-oriented systems design, and local AI agent integrations.

I enjoy combining structural rigor with visual aesthetics. The cosmic theme of this portfolio is inspired by the universe: deep hues, nebulas, and constellations that represent infinite possibilities and curiosity in technology.
